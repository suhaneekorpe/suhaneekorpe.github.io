About Me

I am a PhD student in Computer Science and Statistics at Università Bocconi .

I completed my BS-MS dual degree at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research Bhopal.

My research lies at the intersection of statistical mechanics, information theory, machine learning, and applied mathematics. I am particularly interested in understanding learning and inference in high-dimensional systems using tools from statistical physics and probability.

Research Interests

  • Statistical mechanics of learning and inference
  • High-dimensional statistical inference
  • Information theory
  • Matrix denoising
  • Random matrix theory
  • Phase transitions in machine-learning models

Current Research

My previous work studied symmetric and non-symmetric matrix denoising with non-rotational priors. I combined replica theory and HCIZ integrals to derive free-entropy expressions and used numerical simulations to investigate the corresponding phase diagrams.

I have also studied phase transitions in the binary symmetric perceptron in both the storage and teacher-student settings.

You can find more information on my CV page and my research page.
