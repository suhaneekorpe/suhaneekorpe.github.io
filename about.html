About Me

I am particularly interested in using tools from statistical physics and high-dimensional probability to study inference, learning, and phase transitions in complex systems.

Past Research Projects

Symmetric and Non-Symmetric Matrix Denoising for Non-Rotational Priors

May 2025–April 2026

Supervisor: Dr. Jean Barbier, ICTP

I studied high-rank symmetric and non-symmetric matrix denoising with Rademacher and Gaussian priors. I combined replica theory with HCIZ integrals to derive free-entropy expressions.

I also performed numerical simulations to solve the resulting self-consistent equations, construct phase diagrams, and compare the theoretical predictions with the rotationally invariant estimator.

Phase Transitions in the Binary Symmetric Perceptron

Universidad Complutense de Madrid | May–September 2024

Supervisor: Dr. Aurélien Decelle

I studied the symmetric perceptron in both the storage and teacher-student settings. Using the replica method, I calculated the free energy of the two models.

I used Julia and Python to obtain their phase diagrams and characterize their phase transitions.

Large Language Models

e-Zest Solutions, Pune | December 2023

I studied transformer architectures and explored parameter-efficient fine-tuning of a pretrained Hugging Face language model for translating natural language into graph-database queries.

The Sherrington-Kirkpatrick Model

IISER Bhopal | November 2024

As part of an Advanced Statistical Mechanics course, I conducted a literature review of the foundational Sherrington-Kirkpatrick spin-glass model and presented its theoretical calculations and results to graduate students and faculty.
